The journey from order to delivery

1
Place Your Order
Fill in your jewelry specifications — type, metal, stone details, and any special notes. Upload a reference photo for inspiration.
2
Order Confirmed
Our team reviews your order and confirms the details. You'll see the status update in real time in your portal.
3
CAD Design Created
Our CAD designer creates a 3D model of your piece and uploads it for your review. You'll see a notification in your portal.
4
You Review & Approve
Review the design file, leave feedback, and either approve it, request revisions, or reject. The order moves to manufacturing only after your approval.
5
Manufacturing
Your piece is crafted by our factory. You can watch the status move through VPO Issued → Job Bag → Ready to Invoice → Ready to Ship.
6
Shipped & Delivered
Once shipped, your tracking number appears instantly. The final status update confirms delivery.

Approve Design
You're happy with the design. The order moves directly to manufacturing. This is the final sign-off.
↩
Request Changes
You want adjustments. Write specific feedback in the text box — the CAD designer will revise and reupload.
✕
Reject
The design doesn't match your vision. Our team will follow up to understand your requirements before starting over.
💡 Tips for feedback
• Be specific — "Make the band 20% thinner" is more helpful than "thinner"
• Reference dimensions if you know them — e.g. "2mm band width"
• Mention what you like to keep, not just what to change
• You can request multiple revisions — there's no limit
