Order Lifecycle at a Glance

13 stages from order placement to delivery — each owned by a specific role

1

Order Placed

Customer submits order via portal, or Sales Rep creates it on their behalf. Reference images can be uploaded.

Sales Rep / Customer WAITING_CONFIRMATION
2

Authorized for CAD

Authorizer reviews the order details and approves it to proceed. Order moves to CAD queue.

Authorizer PENDING_CAD
3

CAD Design in Progress

CAD Designer begins work, uploads design files (.jpg, .pdf, .dwg etc). Status updated to reflect active work.

CAD Designer CAD_IN_PROGRESS
4

Design Sent for Customer Approval

CAD Designer marks the file as "Sent for Approval". Customer receives notification and can review, approve, or request changes.

CAD Designer Customer SENT_FOR_APPROVAL
5

Customer Approves Design

Customer clicks "Approve Design" on their portal. Order moves to CUSTOMER_APPROVED. If rejected or revision requested, CAD Designer revises and re-submits.

Customer CUSTOMER_APPROVED
6

SKU Generated

SKU Manager generates a unique product code (format: KJ-[TYPE]-[METAL][COLOR]-[SEQ]). This becomes the product's permanent identifier.

SKU Manager SKU_CREATION
7

VPO Issued

Factory Manager enters the VPO Number and Job Bag Number, issuing a Vendor Purchase Order to the factory.

Factory Manager VPO_ISSUED
8

Job Bag Created / Sent to Contractor

Factory Manager confirms the job bag is created and assigns a vendor/factory. Order enters active production.

Factory Manager ORDER_JOB_BAG_CREATED
9

Manufacturing Complete — Ready to Invoice

Factory Manager clicks "Mark Manufacturing Complete". Order becomes available for invoicing.

Factory Manager READY_TO_INVOICE
10

Ready to Ship

Order appears in the Shipping Manager's "Ready to Ship" queue. Tracking number and carrier are entered.

Shipping Manager READY_TO_SHIP
11

Dispatched / Shipped

Shipping Manager clicks "Dispatch". Tracking number is recorded and customer can see it on their portal.

Shipping Manager SHIPPED
12

Delivered

Shipping Manager marks the order as Delivered. Customer's order shows 100% complete on their portal.

Shipping Manager DELIVERED
💡
Shared feature: Order Conversation — Every order has a built-in messaging thread available to all roles. Staff can mark messages as "Internal" (hidden from customers) or tag colleagues using @mentions. Customers see only public messages.
💡
Kanban Board — Go to /orders/kanban from the Orders page for a visual drag-ready pipeline view of all orders across every stage.

Full Workflow Reference

Every status, who owns it, and what triggers the transition.

# Status Owner Action Required Next Stage
1 WAITING_CONFIRMATION Authorizer Review order specs, click "Authorize" on order detail page PENDING_CAD
2 PENDING_CAD CAD Designer Upload design file in /cad or order detail; move status to CAD_IN_PROGRESS CAD_IN_PROGRESS
3 CAD_IN_PROGRESS CAD Designer In /cad, click "Send for Approval" on the uploaded file CUSTOMER_APPROVED / REJECTED
4 CUSTOMER_APPROVED SKU Manager Go to /sku, find the order, click "+ Generate" to create SKU SKU_CREATION
5 SKU_CREATION Factory Manager In /manufacturing, enter VPO # and Job Bag #, click "Issue VPO & Start" VPO_ISSUED
6 VPO_ISSUED Factory Manager Enter Job Bag # and Vendor name, click "Create Job Bag" ORDER_JOB_BAG_CREATED
7 ORDER_JOB_BAG_CREATED Factory Manager Once production completes, click "Mark Manufacturing Complete" READY_TO_INVOICE / READY_TO_SHIP
8 READY_TO_SHIP Shipping Manager In /shipping, enter tracking number and carrier, click "Dispatch" SHIPPED
9 SHIPPED Shipping Manager Once delivered, click "Mark Delivered" in /shipping DELIVERED ✓

Place New Order — /customer/orders/new

Comprehensive order form with all jewelry specifications. Includes a reference image upload section.

  • Select Order Type (Ring, Pendant, Earrings, Bracelet, etc.)
  • Choose Metal Type (14K, 18K, 10K, Platinum, Silver)
  • Choose Metal Color (Yellow, White, Rose, Two-Tone)
  • Enter stone details (type, quality, shape, carat weight)
  • Upload reference image — drag & drop or click to browse
  • Add special instructions in "Customer Notes"
  • Click "Place Order" to submit
3

Order Detail — /customer/orders/[id]

Full order page with timeline, specs, design files, and team conversation.

  • Status Timeline — Visual dots showing current stage in the 8-step journey
  • Product Specs — All details you ordered (metal, stone, size etc.)
  • Design Files — CAD files uploaded by the designer. When status is "Awaiting Your Approval", action buttons appear
  • Approve Design — Confirms you're happy with the 3D design
  • Request Changes — Sends revision back to designer with your notes
  • Reject — Rejects the design entirely
  • Conversation — Send messages to the team; they reply here
4

Tracking & Delivery

Once shipped, your tracking number appears in the order detail automatically.

  • Tracking number visible in "Order Info" card
  • Progress bar reaches ~92% at SHIPPED
  • 100% shown once Delivered
  • Status badge updates: Shipped → Delivered

Dashboard — /dashboard

KPI overview with real-time metrics and recent orders list.

Full searchable, filterable list of all orders in the system.

Customers — /customers

Manage customer accounts and create orders directly from this page.

  • View all customers with Active/Inactive status

  • Click "+ Order" next to any customer to open the order creation form pre-filled with their info
  • Click "View Orders" to see all orders for that customer
4

Creating a New Order

From either the Orders page or the Customers page.

  • All jewelry spec fields: Order Type, Metal, Color, Size, Stone details
  • Customer fields: Full name, email (auto-filled from customer account)
  • Optional: Quoted Cost, Vendor, Sales Rep email
  • Customer Notes for special instructions
  • After create: option to upload reference image for the designer

CAD Designers receive authorized orders and create 3D design files. They upload files to the system, manage revisions, and send designs for customer approval. They can see their active queue filtered to relevant stages.

1

CAD Files Dashboard — /cad

Your main work area. Shows all CAD files with their current approval status and the upload panel.

  • Left panel: list of all uploaded CAD files with status badges
  • Filter by status: Uploaded, Awaiting Approval, Approved, Rejected, Revision Requested
  • Right panel: Upload a new CAD file for an order
  • KPI cards at top: uploaded count, pending approval, approved, revisions
2

Upload a Design File

Attach a CAD design to a specific order.

  • In the right "Upload CAD File" panel, select the order from dropdown
  • Click "Choose File" to select your design file (.pdf, .jpg, .dwg, etc.)
  • Add optional Designer Notes (visible to customer)
  • Click "Upload" — file appears in the files list
  • Status starts as "Uploaded"
3

Send Design for Customer Approval

Once the design is ready for the customer to review.

  • Find the file in the CAD list
  • Click "→ Send for Approval" button on the file card
  • File status changes to "Awaiting Your Approval"
  • Customer sees the design on their portal with approve/reject/revision buttons
4

Handle Revisions & Rejections

When a customer requests changes or rejects a design.

  • File status changes to "Revision Requested" or "Rejected"
  • Customer's feedback note is visible on the file card
  • Upload a new version of the file (revision number increments automatically)
  • Send new version for approval again
  • Once approved: order moves to CUSTOMER_APPROVED — SKU Manager takes over

SKU Management — /sku

Factory Manager

The Factory Manager oversees all production activity. They issue Vendor Purchase Orders (VPOs), create job bags for factories, track production progress, and mark orders as complete when manufacturing is done.

Manufacturing Queue Issue VPOs Create Job Bags Mark Manufacturing Complete Dashboard
🔑
Test Login (Factory Manager)
Email: arjun.factory@kirajewels.one  ·  Password: test123
1

Manufacturing Dashboard — /manufacturing

Production queue with KPI cards and all active manufacturing orders.

  • 4 KPI cards: Pending Start, VPO Issued, Job Bag Created, Ready to Ship
  • Each order shows current status with a colored left border
  • Actions appear based on each order's current stage
  • Click "Refresh" to reload the queue
2

Issue VPO & Start Production

For orders in SKU_CREATION or CUSTOMER_APPROVED status.

  • Enter VPO Number (e.g. VPO-2026-001)
  • Enter Job Bag Number (e.g. JB-0042)
  • Click "Issue VPO & Start" — status → VPO_ISSUED
3

Create Job Bag

For orders in VPO_ISSUED status.

  • Enter final Job Bag Number
  • Enter Vendor / Factory Name
  • Click "Create Job Bag" — status → ORDER_JOB_BAG_CREATED
  • Order is now in active production at the factory
4

Complete Manufacturing

When the factory delivers the finished piece.

  • Find the order in ORDER_JOB_BAG_CREATED status
  • Click "Mark Manufacturing Complete — Move to Ready to Ship"
  • Status → READY_TO_INVOICE → READY_TO_SHIP
  • Order now appears in Shipping Manager's queue
