    CAD Files — Upload & Review

    How to upload design files, view them, and handle customer approvals.

    For CAD Designers — Uploading a File
    Go to the order, scroll to the Design Files section and click the upload area.
    1

    Open the order you're working on

    Navigate to Orders → find your order (it will be in "Pending CAD" or "CAD In Progress" status) → click to open it.

    2

    Scroll to Design Files

    On the order detail page, scroll past the order specs. You'll see a "Design Files" card. Click the upload area or the Upload button.

    3

    Choose your file and add a note

    Select the CAD file from your computer (SVG, PNG, JPG, or PDF). Add a designer note — explain key design decisions, dimensions, or anything the customer should know.

    Accepted formats: SVG, PNG, JPG, PDF — up to 10MB per file
    4

    Send for Customer Approval

    After uploading, click the "Send for Approval" button on the file. This notifies the customer and marks the file as "Awaiting Your Approval." A message is automatically posted to the conversation.

    • Placing a New Order (as Customer)
    1

    Click "+ Place New Order"

    The new order form opens. Fill in all the fields about your jewelry piece.

    2

    Fill in Jewelry Details

    Choose the type (Engagement Ring, Necklace, etc.), metal type (14K, 18K, Platinum…), metal color (Yellow Gold, White Gold, Rose Gold…), and ring size if applicable.

    3

    Choose the diamond type (Certified Lab Grown Diamond, Non Certified CVD, Non Certified HPHT), quality grade, stone shape, and approximate carat weight.

    A reference image dramatically reduces the number of design revisions needed.
    5

    Add Special Instructions

    Any details the designer needs: engraving text, band width preference, specific setting style, design inspiration, or anything else. The more detail, the better.

    Reviewing Your CAD Design
    When your design is ready, open the order and scroll to "Design Files." You'll see files marked "Awaiting Your Approval."
    1

    Click the 👁 View button

    The design opens in full screen. Take your time — zoom in, look at all the details.

    2

    Read the Designer Note

    The designer explains their decisions below the preview. Read this before giving feedback.

    3

    Make your decision

    ✓ Approve Design — You're happy! Manufacturing begins.
    ↺ Request Changes — Type specific feedback in the box first, then click this. The designer will revise.
    ✕ Reject — The design doesn't work at all. A fresh approach will be started.

    If requesting changes: be specific. "Thinner band" and "more rounded prongs" gets faster results than "doesn't look right."

    Quick Reference

    What to do first day on the job — by role. Bookmark this page.

    First-Day Checklist by Role
    Find your role below for a quick-start guide to using the platform.

    👑 Admin

    You have full access to everything. Start here.

    • Go to Dashboard for an overview
    • Use Pipeline Board to see all active orders by stage
    • To create a user: Customers page → Create User
    • Revenue data is visible only to you on the Dashboard and Order Summary
    • You can move any order to any status using the "Move To" panel
    • Generate AI briefs on any order via the Order Detail page

    🤝 Sales Rep

    You create orders on behalf of clients and track them.

    • Create orders: Orders → + New Order (or Customers page)
    • Always fill in PO Number, Store Name, Metal, and Order Type at minimum
    • Upload reference images when you have them — saves revisions
    • Use the conversation to communicate with customers
    • You can move orders to Waiting Confirmation or Cancelled only

    ⚖️ Authorizer

    You're the quality gate between intake and design.

    • Filter orders by "Waiting Confirmation" to see your queue
    • Open each order → generate AI brief → review specs
    • Click "Authorize Order" to release to CAD team
    • Post a message if you need more info before authorizing
    • You can also approve/reject CAD designs

    ✏️ CAD Designer

    You see only design-stage orders. Here's your flow.

    • Your orders are filtered automatically to CAD stages
    • Open order → read all specs and customer notes carefully
    • Scroll to Design Files → upload your file + add a designer note
    • Click "Send for Approval" when ready for customer review
    • Wait for customer response — check the conversation for feedback
    • If revision requested: upload new file (it becomes Rev #2 automatically)

    🏷️ SKU Manager

    You handle product codes and factory orders.

    • Filter by "Customer Approved" to see your queue
    • Create SKU number for approved designs
    • Issue VPO to move to manufacturing
    • Filter by "SKU Creation" to see pending items

    🏭 Factory Manager

    You track production. Use the Manufacturing page.

    • Go to Manufacturing in the sidebar
    • You see all orders in VPO Issued → Job Bag stages
    • Click an order → use "Move To" to advance through stages
    • When piece passes QC: move to "Ready to Invoice"
    • Post messages to flag any production issues

    🚚 Shipping Manager

    You handle the final mile. Use the Shipping page.

    • Go to Shipping in the sidebar
    • You see all orders in Ready to Invoice → Shipped stages
    • Invoice generated → move to "Ready to Ship"
    • When dispatched: enter tracking number → move to "Shipped"
    • On delivery confirmed: move to "Delivered"

    💍 Customer

    Your portal is simple — here's everything you can do.

    • My Orders shows all your orders with progress bars
    • Click any order to see full details and timeline
    • Design Files section: click 👁 View to see your design
    • Use Approve / Request Changes / Reject on designs
    • Send messages to the team in the Conversation section
    • Tracking number appears in Order Info when shipped

    Common Mistakes to Avoid
    ❌ Don't authorize without checking specs
    Always read the customer notes and verify specs are complete before authorizing. Missing information causes delays later.
    ❌ Don't approve a design too quickly
    Customers: look carefully at the design before approving. Once manufacturing begins, changes are costly and time-consuming.
    ❌ Don't skip the designer note
    CAD Designers: always add a note explaining your design decisions. It reduces revision requests significantly.
    ❌ Don't use vague revision feedback
    Customers: "I don't like it" wastes a revision. Be specific: "Make the band 1mm thinner and add milgrain edges."
    ✅ Do use the conversation for everything
    Every discussion about an order should happen in that order's conversation thread — not email, not WhatsApp. Keep it all in one place.
    ✅ Do use the 📊 Summary page for reviews
    Management and authorizers: the Summary page gives a one-page overview perfect for quick reviews and printed records.
